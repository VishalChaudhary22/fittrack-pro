04 — Seams, not borders
engraved, not outlined
Default seam
Strong (hero)
Active (ember)

Each seam is a pair of inset shadows: a 1px top highlight at 3.5% white and a 1px inner ring at 7% bone. The highlight is what makes it read as a machined edge rather than a CSS outline — and unlike backdrop-filter, it costs nothing to composite.
