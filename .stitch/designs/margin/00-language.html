Margin a design language

Everything the app needs, and nothing that merely decorates it. No cards. No borders. No fills. A single vertical rule holds the page; type and space do the rest. The palette does not change — what changes is how much we're willing to take away.

The name is the instruction: leave space unfilled.

01
The four laws non-negotiable
No containers 0
Vertical rules 1
Ember per screen 1
Section spacing 46px

Cards vanish. Grouping happens through distance and shared baseline, not through boxes.

02
The margin rule 18px

A 1px hairline runs the full height of every screen, 18px from the left edge. Section numbers sit on it. Coach notes hang off it. Hero numerals cross it. Nothing else touches it.

Colour is one channel; the word carries the meaning. Readable in sunlight, by anyone, including a user who can't distinguish red from green.

09
Actions no buttons

Primary actions are typographic bars bounded by hairlines. No fill, no radius, no shadow. The ember one is the screen's single ember.

Gutter note

Notes live in the left gutter at the lightest weight in the system, hanging off the margin rule. They read as marginalia, not as a card with a robot in it.
